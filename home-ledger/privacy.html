Privacy Notice

Application: jk-home-ledger. Last updated 26 July 2026.

1. Controller

The application is operated by a private individual based in Dublin, Ireland, who is also the only data subject. Contact: ethnicore@gmail.com.

2. What data is processed

Account identifiers such as IBAN and account name, account balances, and transaction records including amount, currency, date, counterparty name and reference text. All of it relates to bank accounts held by the operator.

3. Why

Personal budgeting, expense categorisation and household record keeping. The data is not used for profiling of any other person, for marketing, for credit decisions, or for any commercial purpose.

4. Legal basis

Access to the bank is authorised by explicit consent given by the account holder at the bank under PSD2. Because the processing is carried out by an individual in the course of a purely personal or household activity, and concerns only that individual's own data, no processing of other people's data takes place beyond counterparty details that appear inside the operator's own transaction records.

5. Who else is involved

Enable Banking Oy provides the regulated account information service that connects to the bank. The bank itself provides the data. No other processor receives the data. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to advertising or analytics services.

6. Where the data is stored

On private, self-hosted infrastructure in the operator's home in Ireland. Credentials and signing keys are held in encrypted secrets. Transport to and from the API uses TLS. No copy is kept in any public cloud service.

7. How long

Transaction records are kept for as long as they are useful for personal accounting and are deleted on request of the operator. Bank access consent itself expires no later than 180 days after it is granted and must be renewed with strong customer authentication.

8. Rights and withdrawal

Consent to bank access can be withdrawn at any time through the bank's own channels, which immediately ends all further data retrieval. The stored data can be deleted in full at any time by the operator.
